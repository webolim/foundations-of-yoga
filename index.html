Answer: The complete line is from the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali (2.29): Yama niyama asana pranayama pratyahara dharana dhyana samadhayo - ashtavangani. These are the eight limbs of yoga listed by Patanjali.

Answer: These are the five kleshas (afflictions) described in the Yoga Sutras — Avidya (ignorance), Asmita (egoism), Raga (attachment), Dvesha (aversion), Abhinivesha (clinging to life / fear of death). Their significance is that they are the root causes of suffering and obstacles to liberation; yoga practices aim to reduce and remove them.

  • Avidya: Misunderstanding of the true nature of reality — the root klesha.
  • Asmita: Identification of the Self with ego-mind processes.
  • Raga: Attachment to pleasurable experiences.
  • Dvesha: Aversion to unpleasant experiences.
  • Abhinivesha: Fear of death and clinging to life.
